Helping our feathered friends:
how we can support birds?

Thanks to birds:

  • damage from pests is reduced by 20–30%
  • one owl can eat about 1,000 mice per season
  • a hectare of field saves around $100–$200 annually
ORDER
  • Birds consume a huge amount of harmful insects, protecting plants from pests and reducing the need for chemicals
  • Many species disperse seeds, contributing to forest restoration and increasing biodiversity
  • Being part of food chains, they maintain stability in natural communities
  • Birdwatching reduces stress, improves mood, and boosts overall vitality
  • The health of bird populations often reflects air quality, water quality, and general well-being of nature
